The Faculty & Doctors

Jakir Hossain Medical College & Research Institute has a team of over 250 qualified faculty members across all major medical disciplines, including Professors, Associate Professors, Assistant Professors and Consultants. Our academic staff is committed to high-quality teaching, clinical training and community healthcare in Murshidabad and West Bengal.

We follow a structured, competency-based curriculum aligned with NMC guidelines, ensuring students receive a strong academic foundation supported by ongoing professional development activities.

Admission Procedure

MBBS Programme @ Jakir Hossain Medical College

The MBBS programme at Jakir Hossain Medical College & Research Institute is designed to develop competent, compassionate and ethically responsible medical professionals who are capable of addressing healthcare needs at community, regional and national levels. The curriculum emphasizes structured academic learning, clinical exposure, patient-centered training and continuous self-learning in accordance with the latest NMC guidelines.

Admission Eligibility & Criteria

Admission to the MBBS course at Jakir Hossain Medical College and Rearch Institute conducted strictly through the Undergraduate National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG). All seats are allotted through the centralized counselling process conducted by The West Bengal University of Health Sciences (WBUHS), Govt. of West Bengal, based on NEET-UG merit ranking.
